Trade ideas

Concrete plans built from the strongest news setups: where to get in, where to get out if wrong (stop), where to take profit (target), and how long the news usually keeps working. Every target is 2× the risk, so you only need to win more than 1 in 3 to come out ahead. The win rate shown is measured from this app's own past trade ideas. Until enough have played out, it says so instead of guessing.

Search any US-listed stock in the box at the top (e.g. AAPL or Palantir). You'll get a verdict on whether the news and other evidence point up or down, and why.

Track record

Every scan logs its signals with the price at the time. This page shows what actually happened 1, 5 and 20 trading days later. "Right" means a bullish signal went up or a bearish one went down. It takes a few days of scans before results appear, and needs weeks before the numbers mean much.

Updates

Market Radar updates itself automatically: it checks for a new version on launch and every 3 hours, downloads it in the background, and switches to it the next time you restart.

Data sources

News: CNBC, MarketWatch, Seeking Alpha, Investing.com, FXStreet, PR Newswire and targeted Google News searches. Prices, movers and earnings: Nasdaq public data. Economic calendar: ForexFactory. All free — no accounts or API keys. Feeds can rate-limit; a failed source is skipped and retried next refresh.

Signals are automated news analysis, not financial advice or a prediction. Always read the source articles and manage your own risk.
