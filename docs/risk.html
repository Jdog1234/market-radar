Risk Disclosure

Last updated November 24, 2026

Trading stocks involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for everyone. You can lose some or all of the money you invest. Only trade with money you can afford to lose.

Market Radar is not advice

Market Radar is software that analyses news and prices automatically. Its signals, scores, "Before the move" lists, trade ideas, entries, stops, targets, position sizes and stock lookups are generated by rules, not by a licensed financial adviser, and they don't consider your finances, goals or risk tolerance. Market Radar is not a broker-dealer or investment adviser. Consider speaking to a licensed professional before making investment decisions.

Signals can be wrong

  • News can be misread, attributed to the wrong company, late, or already reflected in the price.
  • Data from third-party sources can be delayed, incomplete or incorrect.
  • Research-based rules describe past tendencies on average. They can and do fail on individual stocks and in different market conditions.
  • The app's track record and win rates describe past ideas. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Order and execution risks

  • Stop-loss orders don't guarantee a price: a stock can gap past your stop, especially at the open or after news.
  • Market orders can fill at worse prices than shown, particularly in fast markets or for less-traded stocks.
  • Short selling can lose more than the amount invested; margin increases both gains and losses.
  • Internet, app, data or broker outages can delay or prevent orders or cancellations.

Practice versus real trading

The simulated account and Alpaca's paper account don't account for every real-world effect (slippage, partial fills, fees, liquidity, emotions). Real results are usually worse than practice results.

Your brokerage account

Real orders go to your own account at Alpaca, which is your broker. Market Radar doesn't hold your money and never places orders without your action. You're responsible for every order, for monitoring your positions at Alpaca, and for keeping your API keys secure. Market Radar's confirmation prompts and size limit are safety aids, not guarantees.
