Market Radar

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Market Radar is built to collect as little about you as possible. The app runs on your own computer and doesn't use accounts, tracking or analytics.

What stays on your computer

  • Your settings, watchlist, practice-trading account, alerts, drawings and scan history are saved only on your computer.
  • Your Pro license key and any broker API keys are encrypted with your computer's own secure storage (Keychain on Mac, the Windows equivalent on Windows).
  • We don't receive copies of any of this.

Who the app talks to, and why

  • Public news and market-data sources (news sites and feeds, exchanges and data providers): to download headlines, prices and charts. Like any website visit, they see your IP address.
  • Gumroad: if you buy Pro, Gumroad collects your email and payment details to process the purchase (see Gumroad's privacy policy). The app sends your license key to Gumroad to check that your subscription is active, about twice a day.
  • Alpaca: only if you connect a brokerage account. Your API keys and the orders you place are sent directly from your computer to Alpaca. Alpaca's own privacy policy applies to your brokerage account.
  • GitHub: the app checks GitHub for updates and downloads new versions from it.
  • Polygon.io: only if you add your own Polygon key for licensed data.

What we receive

As the seller, Gumroad shows us your name or email, what you bought and when, so we can provide support and refunds. We don't sell or share it, and we don't use it for advertising.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Both may log standard request data such as your IP address. The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Your choices

  • Remove your license key or broker keys in the app at any time; deleting the app's data folder removes everything it stored.
  • To ask about or delete the purchase information we hold, contact us through your Gumroad receipt.

Children

Market Radar isn't directed at children, and real trading requires a brokerage account, which has its own age requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
