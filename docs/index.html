Everything a news-driven trader checks, in one place

Built on published research: earnings drift, analyst revisions, insider buying, short interest, and how prices under-react to news.

Before the move

Strong news, weak reaction. Market Radar measures how far each stock has moved since its news against its normal daily range, and surfaces the ones that haven't caught up yet.

  • Guidance raises, upgrades, FDA approvals, offerings, short reports
  • Companies reporting soon with a consistent beat or miss streak

Trade ideas with the plan built in

Every idea comes with an entry, a stop-loss sized to the stock's normal move, a 2:1 target, a time frame based on the catalyst, and a position size for your account.

  • Win rates are measured from past ideas, never invented
  • One click loads the plan into the trading screen

Practice, then trade for real

A TradingView-style trading screen with live streaming prices. Practice in the built-in simulated account, then connect your own Alpaca brokerage account and send real orders from the same charts.

  • Up to four live charts, drawing tools and indicators
  • Drag stop-loss and take-profit lines; breakeven and trailing stops in one click
  • Every real-money order asks you to confirm, with a size limit you set

Look up any stock

Type a ticker and get a verdict on which way the evidence points: news catalysts, trend, analyst consensus, earnings history, insider buying and short interest, each shown with its weight.

Learn to trade, with real data

A full trading school, a pattern library and a game built on real charts.

  • Trading school: 10 modules, from your first order to liquidity, risk and psychology, with hands-on simulators (order book, order types, position sizing, 100-trade expectancy), a trade replay demo on real charts, practice missions and quizzes
  • Candle puzzles: real charts cut off at a pattern. Name it, call what happened next, earn XP and level up
  • Pattern library: 22 candlestick patterns, each with its real track record across ~50,000 trading days

Price alerts

Set an alert on any price, right on the chart. You get a desktop notification even when the app is in the background.

Size by risk

Type how much of your account you're willing to lose if the stop is hit, and the share count is worked out for you.

Your colors

Seven themes including Light, any accent color, and a color-blind-friendly candle set.

No fake odds

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Apple M1/M2/M3/M4" means Apple Silicon.

Mac: "can't be verified" message

Market Radar isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet, so macOS asks first.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Market Radar into Applications.
  2. Open it. If macOS warns you, click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

Windows: "Windows protected your PC"

SmartScreen shows this for new apps without a code-signing certificate.

  1. Run the installer.
  2. Click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway.
